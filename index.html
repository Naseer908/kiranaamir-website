Clinical Focus & Conditions Treated

Disorders We Deal With

Specialized, evidence-based psychological treatment and hypnotherapeutic intervention tailored to address complex mental health conditions.

Trauma & PTSD

Trauma is any experience and event that wounds a person. It can be psychological and or physical. The significant emotional and psychological distress leads to impaired personal, social and occupational functioning that result in trauma and stress related disorders. Common known terms are Post traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and Adjustment disorder.

Eating Disorders

It is specified by irregular, impulsive and uncontrolled eating patterns with underlying emotional disturbance that demand clinical attention. Commonly known terms include Binge eating, Purging, Anorexia and Bulimia Nervosa..

Personality Disorders

This cluster is identified by set of personality characteristics, habits, preferences and behaviors that make person a misfit to get along with others and impacts social and occupational functioning. Commonly known terms include Suspicious Personalities, Avoidant, Antisocial, Dependent, Narcissistic and Obsessive, Compulsive Personality Disorder.

Abuse-Related Disorders

Compassionate care for survivors of physical, psychological, domestic, or emotional abuse to heal deep wounds and regain empowerment.

Anxiety & Panic Disorders

Effective relief from chronic anxiety, panic attacks, social phobias, and persistent worry using CBT, Hypnotherapy, and NLP.

Depression & Mood Disorders

Structured treatment for major depression, persistent sadness, low motivation, and emotional exhaustion to reignite hope.

Psycho-Sexual Disorders

A wide range of dysfunctions that impair subject's sexual feelings, desires, thoughts and behavior caused by psychological conditions. Gender identity issues, Paraphilias, and sexual dysfunction are common among the list.

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder

Behavioral reframing and hypnotherapy to interrupt intrusive thoughts and reduce repetitive compulsive behaviors.

Suicide & Crisis Intervention

Suicide is a phenomenon that happens in every corner of the world. Suicide includes ideation and active act of deliberately ending one's own life. Crisis intervention involves immediate, short term management in the face of dire challenges to ensure safety and stability of the correspondent.

01 / Rogerian Therapy

Client Centered Therapy (CCT)

A non-directive, compassionate therapeutic approach built on unconditional positive regard and empathetic listening. Focuses on helping clients reconnect with their authentic self and unlock intrinsic emotional healing and growth.

02 / Cognitive Therapy

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

A structured, evidence-based modality focusing on identifying and reframing negative thought patterns and emotional triggers. Equips clients with practical coping skills for anxiety, depression, and daily stressors.

03 / Rational Emotive

Rational Emotive Behavioral Therapy (REBT)

An action-oriented approach aimed at actively challenging irrational beliefs and rigid demands. Helps individuals replace self-defeating thoughts with rational alternatives to foster emotional self-regulation.

04 / Internal Systems

Internal Family Systems (IFS) Therapy

An integrative model that views the mind as comprised of distinct internal sub-personalities ("parts") led by a core Self. Promotes inner harmony, self-compassion, and resolution of deep internal conflicts.

05 / Experiential

Gestalt Therapy

A holistic, experiential therapy emphasizing present-moment awareness, bodily sensations, and personal responsibility. Helps resolve unfinished emotional business and cultivate personal authenticity.

06 / Couples & Partners

Relationship & Couples Therapy

Specialized relational counselling designed to rebuild healthy communication, resolve recurring interpersonal friction, address intimacy concerns, and restore emotional connection between partners.

07 / Family Dynamics

Family Therapy

Systemic sessions involving family members to enhance relationship dynamics, establish healthy emotional boundaries, and navigate complex household or intergenerational challenges.

08 / Specialized Health

Psycho-Sexual Disorders Therapy

Highly sensitive, confidential therapy addressing psychological factors behind sexual dysfunctions, desires, identity questions, paraphilias, and related emotional distress.

09 / Psychodynamic

Psychoanalytical Therapy

An in-depth therapeutic exploration of unconscious drives, early childhood experiences, and defense mechanisms to bring buried emotional patterns into conscious clarity and lasting resolution.

10 / Trauma Recovery

Trauma Focused Therapy

Targeted psychological care for physical or emotional events that wound the individual. Focuses on resolving Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) and Adjustment Disorders to restore personal safety.

11 / Acute Care

Crisis Intervention

Immediate, short-term emergency management during severe psychological distress or suicidal ideation to re-establish emotional stability, immediate coping mechanisms, and safety.

12 / Subconscious Care

Hypnotherapy

NGH-certified subconscious therapy utilizing safe trance states to directly modify stubborn habits, phobias, sleep disturbances, and deep-rooted anxiety responses.

13 / Mindset Transformation

Neuro-linguistic Programming (NLP)

NFNLP-certified cognitive and behavioral techniques designed to rapidly reframe mental representations, alter unwanted behaviors, and instill lasting personal confidence.

14 / Diagnostic

Psycho-diagnostic Interviewing

A structured clinical interview process designed to understand the client's psychological history, current concerns, and diagnostic presentation. Forms the foundation for accurate assessment and treatment planning.

15 / Assessment

Psychological Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation using standardized psychological tools to measure cognitive, emotional, and behavioral functioning. Includes detailed report writing and clinical recommendations for treatment.

16 / Residential

Inpatient Facility

Residential mental health care for clients requiring intensive, round-the-clock therapeutic support and supervision. Provides a safe, structured environment for stabilization, recovery, and long-term rehabilitation under professional clinical care.

17 / Cognitive

Cognitive Disorders

It involves deficits in mental processing, thinking, planning and judgment. Commonly known terms include Memory related disturbance, Dementia, Delirium, Alzheimer's and difficulties related to focus, attention and concentration.

18 / Addiction

Addiction Treatment

It is characterized by craving, physiological and psychological dependency on a substance of abuse (prescribed and non-prescribed). It significantly impairs mental, physical health and social, occupational functioning of an individual.
